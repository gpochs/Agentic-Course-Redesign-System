# Assessment and release security

- Classify student-facing assessments, teacher-only keys/model answers,
  rubrics/marking schemes, grading records, and public-safe outputs separately.
- Never infer that a dated, partial, or combined test/key is current, complete,
  or safe for students.
- Require separate exact student-facing and lecturer-only production targets.
- Before release, scan visible text, editable layers, comments, notes, metadata,
  alt text, hidden objects, speaker notes, and derived PDFs for answers, model
  responses, teacher comments, internal QA text, and secure-bank leakage.
- Do not treat AI output as evidence of student competence. Keep task-level AI
  permissions, independent evidence, verification, attribution, accessibility,
  and validity explicit.
- A public-safe output requires separate rights, privacy, factual, and audience
  clearance. No workflow stage publishes or distributes materials.
