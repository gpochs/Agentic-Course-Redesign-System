# Synthetic assessment policy

- One individual decision brief: 1,500 words, worth 70 percent.
- One individual oral defence: 8 minutes, worth 30 percent.
- Pass threshold: 50 percent overall.
- No compensation rule or rounding rule is supplied in this fixture.
- Generative AI may support brainstorming, but claims and sources must be checked.
- The submitted brief and oral defence must demonstrate the learner's own
  judgement.

Unknown grading and moderation details must remain explicit questions rather
than invented rules.
