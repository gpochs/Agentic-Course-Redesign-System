## Agentic Course Redesign

Redesign a course together

Start with one teaching goal, a single lesson or a whole course. Choose “Skip for now” to continue without opening files. You can finish the redesign this way. If you decide to use a private document or an online course later, you choose what to share.

A syllabus or module outline can answer questions about the programme, learners, outcomes and assessment. The conversation builds on what you share and asks one question at a time. Describe accessibility needs and approved exam arrangements in terms of what the course must support.

Ten specialists help you check evidence, plan teaching and assessment, and make materials. Choose a focus, compare suggestions and explain your own priorities in your own words. You can combine ideas, request changes or set an idea aside. You approve the plan and the exact materials before work starts.

Combine local or cloud documents, a learning platform and teaching apps as needed. You can share one item or agree on a batch. When a tool would help, you can choose it or continue with the tools already available.

Review editable slides, handouts, spreadsheets, quizzes, web pages or online course activities using the tools available to you. Open a working copy, browser preview or student view, then decide which items to accept, revise or reject.
