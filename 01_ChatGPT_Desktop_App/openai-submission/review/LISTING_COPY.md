## Agentic Course Redesign

Redesign a course together

Start with one teaching goal, a single lesson or a whole course. Choose “Skip for now” to start without opening course files. You can work all the way to finished materials this way. When an existing document or private LMS becomes relevant, the orchestrator asks about that material at the point of use.

Build a short course brief together. The orchestrator uses what you already know about the study programme, module, learners, outcomes and assessment. It asks one question when the answer will change a design decision. You may describe an approved accessibility or exam adjustment at course level and leave personal student details out.

Ten specialists map outcomes, check research, design activities and assessments, and review workload, accessibility and feasibility. You choose the focus, compare recommendations, approve the blueprint and file plan, and accept or revise each finished item. The orchestrator asks one question at a time and leaves room for your own answer.

Create editable slides, handouts, spreadsheets, quizzes, web pages or draft LMS activities using the tools and access available in your session. Review a working copy, browser preview or student view before deciding what to keep. You control the sources, tools and destinations.
