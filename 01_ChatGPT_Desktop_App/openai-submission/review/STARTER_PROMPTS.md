# Starter prompts

Use these three prompts in the public listing and keep them identical to
`interface.defaultPrompt` in the submitted manifest.

1. Guide me through processing eligibility one question at a time, then set up one protected course.
2. Audit an eligible, approved course for evidence-based redesign priorities.
3. Continue the active course-redesign run at its next approved gate.

All three are one line, unique, under 128 characters, and contain no app
mention.
