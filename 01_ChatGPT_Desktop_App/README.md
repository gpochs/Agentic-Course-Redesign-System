# Agentic Course Redesign for ChatGPT and Codex

Start a conversation with **Agentic Course Redesign** and describe one thing you want learners to do better. You can redesign one quiz, a course module or an entire course. This folder contains version **0.3.2**. Check the listing for the version currently offered by OpenAI. [OpenAI plugin directory](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugins_6a88cdd968088191b7a22c9e92cbb5ba)

In **Step 1 · Start with your course**, choose any mix of a learning platform, local or cloud files, learning apps or other sources. You can add a description or choose **Skip for now** to start with your teaching goal. You can still finish the redesign and receive actual materials. If you add a private source later, the plugin checks that item when you need it. [Follow the nine teaching steps](plugins/agentic-course-redesign/assets/PARTICIPANT_QUICK_START.md)

## What to share first

Describe what you want learners to do and what you already know about the course. A syllabus or module description may already answer questions about the study programme, prerequisites, outcomes, workload and assessment. A short summary works too. The next question builds on your answers and asks about something that would change the design.

For a timed exam, it may ask which rules or approved adjustments the design needs to support. Describe the requirement, such as extra time or an equivalent response format, at course level. Keep names, diagnoses and individual accommodation decisions private. You can continue with independent design work while a local rule is being checked.

## Working with the specialists

You work in one conversation and answer one teaching question at a time. After a first look at the course, you choose what to focus on. Ten specialists examine outcomes, activities, AI use, student experience, assessment, research quality, feasibility, learning design, material production and accessibility. Their recommendations identify the change, supporting evidence and what students or staff would do differently. You choose which recommendations to include and approve the plan before anyone makes drafts.

Try each draft in the form your learners will see. For slides, inspect the presentation view. For a quiz, attempt a sample question. For a web page or a learning-platform activity, open the learner view where your permissions allow. You can accept an item, request a change or leave it out.
